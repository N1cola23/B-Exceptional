Escort Services

B-Exceptional is strongly committed to providing cross-border services. We offer professional escorting for exceptional transports throughout Europe.

Each European country has its own rules and procedures regarding transport escorting. Thanks to close cooperation with our sister companies in the Netherlands and Germany, we can efficiently and professionally support cross-border transports. In certain cases, our staff can also escort in other countries, such as France, Denmark, or Switzerland.

Whether escorting is required depends on the dimensions of the transport in many countries. Germany is an exception: escorting is specified in the exemption permit, and requirements differ per federal state. Germany uses BF2, BF3, and BF4 escort vehicles. Our sister company operates all three types and can also arrange HiPo (Hilfepolizei) and police escorting. Several Dutch-speaking escorts are certified to operate in Germany as BF2 or BF3.
