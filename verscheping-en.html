Shipping

B-Exceptional focuses strongly on offering cross-border services, including solutions for overseas transport. To support transport companies as much as possible, B-Exceptional has expanded its service package to include shipping arrangements.

Common shipments include crossings to the United Kingdom from ports in the Netherlands, Belgium, and France. Shipments to Scandinavian countries are also familiar territory for us.

For these and many other destinations, you can request a non-binding quotation.
