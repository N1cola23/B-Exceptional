Route Surveys

During a route survey, we analyze the complete routes that your exceptional transport needs to follow. We inspect turns, roundabouts, clearance heights, widths, bridges, tunnels, and local restrictions. Based on this analysis, we determine whether the transport can be carried out safely and legally.

If necessary, we provide alternative routes, assist with applying for local permits, and ensure proper signage and escort services.
