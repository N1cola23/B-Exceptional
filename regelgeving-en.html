Netherlands – Permits

A permit for indivisible loads is required from: 22.00 x 3.00 x 4.00 x 50,000 kg.

In the Netherlands, a distinction is made between a long-term permit and an incidental permit.

Long-term permit

A long-term permit can be applied for using the registration number of a tractor unit or trailer. In the Netherlands, the long-term permit is valid for one year. The maximum dimensions are:

  • Tractor unit: 22.00 x 3.50 x 4.25 x 50,000 kg.
  • Trailer: 27.50 x 3.50 x 4.25 x 100,000 kg.

For a long-term permit, the maximum dimensions depend on the technical capabilities of the vehicle.

Incidental permit

If a long-term permit is not sufficient, an incidental permit can be applied for. An incidental permit is valid for a minimum of four and a maximum of twelve weeks.

Escorts

Self-propelled machinery

Different regulations apply to self-propelled machinery, such as mobile cranes. In this case, a permit is required if:

  • Width > 3.00 m.
  • Length > 20.00 m.
  • Axle load > 12,000 kg.
  • Mass > 60,000 kg.

Transport escort is required if the mass exceeds 84,000 kg.

Entire Belgium

An Entire Belgium permit can, as the name suggests, be used throughout Belgium.

Motorway networks

In Belgium, motorways may be used with a maximum mass of 44,000 kg and a maximum height of 4.30 m.

Network 90 or 120

Belgium works with network permits. Specific network routes have been established which may be used by transport with a Network 90 or 120 permit. These routes run throughout Belgium and include, for example, the ports of Antwerp and Zeebrugge.

Route

A route permit can be applied for if the transport dimensions exceed the above dimensions, or if the final destination is outside the network.

Crane network

In addition to the permits mentioned above, Belgium also has a Crane Network permit for the transport of self-propelled machinery.

On days preceding a public holiday, all exceptional transport is prohibited from 16:00 until 24:00 on the public holiday. In addition, all exceptional transport is prohibited from Saturday 12:00 until Sunday 24:00.

Germany – Permits

A permit for indivisible loads is required from: 16.50 x 2.55 x 4.00 x 40,000 kg.

For a motor vehicle with trailer, a permit for indivisible loads is required from: 18.75 x 2.55 x 4.00 x 40,000 kg.

In Germany, permits are distinguished according to different legal paragraphs.

If the vehicle complies with the legally permitted maximum dimensions but the load is too long, too high or too wide, a permit according to §46 StVZO (Straßenverkehrs-Zulassungs-Ordnung) is required.

If a vehicle exceeds the permitted maximum dimensions and/or weights, both a permit according to §70 StVZO and a permit according to §29 and §46 StVZO are required.

A §70 is a vehicle approval for vehicles or combinations that may exceed the legal maximum dimensions. The basis for granting a §70 is a TÜV Gutachten. A TÜV Gutachten contains the technical data of the vehicle combination. A TÜV Gutachten is often provided when purchasing a new trailer. You can also obtain one through NL Exceptioneel.

Based on a §70, a permit according to §29 can be applied for. A §29 permit is an incidental permit valid for a maximum of three months.

The price of a German §29 permit is determined on the basis of seven different factors. Not all of these factors can be determined in advance. For a non-binding indicative price for your permit, please contact our permits department.

For German permits, we work closely with our German sister company. This allows us to make maximum use of the knowledge of the German road network available within the NL Exceptioneel group. Cooperation with the German authorities is also very important.

Escorts

Germany has no fixed regulations regarding transport escort. Whether transport escort is required, and in what form, depends on the requirements stated in the permit. These requirements may differ between federal states. The requirements depend on various factors, such as the dimensions of the transport or roadworks.

Germany has different levels of transport escort. A permit may specify BF2, BF3, BF4 or Hilfepolizei.

Our sister company in Germany has BF2, BF3 and BF4 vehicles and can also organise HiPo (Hilfepolizei) and police escorts. Some Dutch NL Exceptioneel escorts are also trained to operate in Germany as BF2 or BF3.

Driving times

In Germany, driving times for exceptional transport are specified in the permit. Driving times may differ between federal states.

Guidage

Guidage refers to escorting by private escorts on yellow motorcycles. If no guideurs are available, the police may escort the transport. Police escort may also be required based on the permit.

Driving times

Driving times in France may differ by department and motorway. Throughout France, exceptional transport is prohibited from Saturday, or the day preceding a public holiday, at 12:00 until Monday, or the day following a public holiday, at 06:00. Driving is prohibited during snowfall, icy conditions or poor visibility.

Austria – Permits

A permit for indivisible loads is required from: 16.50 x 2.55 x 4.00 x 40,000 kg.

For a motor vehicle with trailer, a permit for indivisible loads is required from: 18.75 x 2.55 x 4.00 x 40,000 kg.

Annual permit: The maximum dimensions of an annual permit for Austria are: 25.00 x 3.50 x 4.20 x 60,000 kg.

An annual permit is not issued for all federal states. A separate application is required for Tyrol and Vorarlberg.

An annual permit contains a road network. The annual permit is only valid on the roads included in it.

If the transport does not fall within the dimensions specified here, please contact us without obligation for more information about an incidental permit.

Escorts – Stufen

  • Stufe 1: The transport must be accompanied by one transport escort.
  • Stufe 2: Certified Austrian transport escort.
  • Stufe 3: Two certified escorts.
  • Stufe 4: Three certified escorts + escort according to Stufe 1.

Norway – Permits

In Norway, a permit is required if the following dimensions are exceeded: 20.00 x 3.25 x 4.50 x 50t.

Norway does not have long-term permits. Only route permits are issued, with a validity of one month.

Escorts

From a width of three metres and/or a length of 23.50 metres, at least one escort is required in Norway. The exact number of escorts is not known in advance and is determined by the authorities when the permit is issued.

Sweden – Permits

In Sweden, a permit is required if a transport is wider than 3.50 metres or higher than 4.50 metres. A permit is also required for a length of at least 30 metres. If the transport is longer than 24 metres and at least one other legally permitted dimension is also exceeded, a permit is likewise required.

Regarding weight, a permit is required from 42,000 kg or 48,000 kg. For a five-axle combination, a permit is required from 42,000 kg. For combinations with six or more axles, a permit is required from 48,000 kg.

Sweden does not have long-term permits. Only route permits are issued, with a validity of up to three months.

Escorts

In Sweden, in addition to regular transport escort, VTL escort is also used. VTL stands for Vägtransportledare and can be considered a replacement for the police. The table below shows when escort and/or VTL is required.

Switzerland distinguishes between two types of permits: an annual permit and an incidental permit. The maximum dimensions of an annual permit are 30.00 x 3.00 x 4.00 x 40,000 kg.
